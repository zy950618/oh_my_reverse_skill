# Fingerprint diagnostics skills-experience

This directory is a lightweight index of representative skills-experience cards for public fingerprint diagnostics.

It is not the formal evidence store. Formal evidence and validation artifacts belong in:

- `public-range-evidence/`
- tool reports and ledgers
- scope contracts such as `configs/range_scope_contract.yaml`
- evals that encode the expected behavior and safety boundary

Tracked cards here should stay small and representative:

- keep a compact multi-target matrix sample;
- keep at most a current smoke sample when it adds value;
- do not track every run;
- do not store raw browser profiles, cookies, storage, HAR bodies, tokens, or private headers here.

Public diagnostics such as Sannysoft, CreepJS, BrowserLeaks, and Incolumitas are observation-only. Their results must not be claimed as WAF/challenge bypass, fingerprint evasion, production readiness, or third-party site success.
